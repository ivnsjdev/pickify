# Pickify

Pickify is a randomizer for iPhone and iPad: 18 playful ways to pick a name, split a team,
or settle a decision. Spin a wheel, roll real 3D dice, flip a coin, draw the shortest
straw, or put everyone's finger on the screen and let the app choose.

Its games run on your device. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics, and no
tracking, and an internet connection is only needed when Apple services are used to start
the free trial or buy the one-time unlock, or when you choose to open an external link.
